Intelligent control of long-distance single-lane two-way traffic

This research addresses coordinated passage of opposing vehicle platoons on mountain construction access roads through vehicle detection and signal control. The control system developed by the team has been deployed at an expressway construction site.

TYPE
Traffic control, a team research project
STATUS
Deployed at a construction site
WHEN

WHO
Joint work with the team

Research problem

Mountain expressway construction access roads often operate as long-distance single-lane two-way roads. Opposing vehicles can pass only at designated passing bays. Simultaneous entry into the same single-lane section can cause a conflict and require reversing. The research objective is to coordinate opposing platoons so that they meet within the passing bays.

Control method

Each single-lane section between adjacent passing bays is treated as a control section. Vehicle detection at both ends identifies section occupancy and travel direction, while signal control coordinates alternating entry by opposing platoons. A waiting platoon remains in a passing bay until the opposing platoon has passed (fig. 1).

Coordination of platoon passage places opposing encounters at the passing bays. Figure 2 illustrates the resulting time-space relationships.
